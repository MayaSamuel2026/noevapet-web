# NoevaPet V13

Baseline: NoevaPet PreDeployment V12.

Changes in V13:
- Replaced header/footer/favicons with latest supplied NoevaPet full-colour logo system.
- New distinctive 8-icon Pet Match family matching the teal/sand/coral brand palette.
- Enlarged/high-contrast orbit nodes so icons remain legible in the routine wheel.
- My Pets gains salmon/sage contrast, an editorial sketch, and clearer selected-state hierarchy.
- Basket gains an active-pet photo panel, stronger flat-colour step hierarchy, 4 dedicated process icons, an illustrated empty-cart state, and higher contrast through all purchase stages.
- Results and comparison screens gain stronger flat-colour surfaces and small editorial graphic cues.
- Application data/interaction logic retained; only icon asset mapping and empty-state markup were changed.
