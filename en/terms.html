Terms of use — pre-deployment draft.

This draft describes the intended NoevaPet model and requires legal review before launch.

1. Role of NoevaPet

NoevaPet provides information, comparisons, recommendations and links to external retailers. NoevaPet is not the seller of products bought from those retailers.

2. Purchase contract

Any purchase contract for linked products is concluded only between the user and the relevant retailer. Prices, availability, shipping, payment, returns and warranty are governed by the retailer.

3. Recommendations

Recommendations are intended to make product choice easier and do not replace individual veterinary advice, particularly for health-related questions.

4. Affiliate links

NoevaPet may receive a commission for referred purchases. This is disclosed transparently.

5. Availability and accuracy

Product, price and availability information can change. The retailer’s information is authoritative at checkout.

6. Status

Pre-deployment draft, 16 September 2026. Legal review required before production release.
