Clearly explained

How NoevaPet makes money — and why that should be visible.

Trust works only when it is clear who pays whom and when.

Comparison comes first

NoevaPet compares and explains. The retailer sells.

Commission on referred purchases

If a tracked retailer link leads to a purchase, NoevaPet may receive a commission.

Your price should stay the same

The commission should not increase the price you pay at the retailer.

How we handle possible conflicts

An affiliate model can create a conflict of interest. That is why recommendation logic should remain separate from commercial compensation. In the live system, retailer compensation, availability and recommendation logic should be kept technically and editorially distinct.

What we do not promise

NoevaPet is not the retailer, does not take payment for retailer purchases, and does not handle delivery or returns. Those remain with the retailer.

NoevaPet helps you find products that fit your pet, understand the differences and move to the retailer with one clear shopping plan.
