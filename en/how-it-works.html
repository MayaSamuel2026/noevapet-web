Good to know

Straight answers for a first purchase.

Do I buy from NoevaPet?

No. You complete your purchase with the retailer.

Do I have to search again?

No. Where possible, we prepare the retailer cart; otherwise we open the exact products.

Why this option?

Because it best matches your pet and your chosen balance of price and convenience.

How does NoevaPet make money?

We may earn a retailer commission on tracked purchases, normally at no extra cost to you.
