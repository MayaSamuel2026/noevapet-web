Privacy information — pre-deployment draft.

This page is structurally complete for the pre-deployment build. Mandatory legal details, actual service providers and final contact details must be checked against the production environment before launch.

2. Data that may be processed

Depending on use, this may include contact data, pet-profile information, uploaded pet photos, technical usage data and information about selected products. In this static pre-deployment build, pet profiles and shopping-plan demos are stored only in the browser.

3. Purposes

Processing is intended to provide the website, personalise the selection flow, save optional pet profiles, handle contact requests and — after launch — measure referred retailer visits.

4. Pet photos

Uploaded photos make pet profiles personal. Before launch, the production storage location, retention period and deletion process must be added.

5. Retailer links and affiliate tracking

Tracked retailer links may process technical attribution data so referral commissions can be recognised. The actual providers and legal bases must be inserted before launch.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The pre-deployment build uses browser local storage for demo interactions. The final consent setup must match the services actually used in production.

7. Rights

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection and the right to complain to a supervisory authority.

8. Status

Pre-deployment draft, 16 September 2026. Legal review required before production release.
