FAQ

The questions people actually have before a first purchase.

Answered plainly, without hiding the important part in small print.

Is NoevaPet a shop?

No. NoevaPet helps with discovery, comparison and shopping planning. You buy and pay with the retailer.

Do I need a NoevaPet account?

Not necessarily for browsing. A profile becomes useful when you want to reuse your pets and their details.

Will I still need a retailer account?

Possibly. If the retailer requires a login, you sign in directly with them. NoevaPet never asks for the retailer password.

Does NoevaPet track my delivery?

Not in V1. Delivery and tracking remain with the retailer.

Why does NoevaPet show so few products?

Because less choice is often more useful. The goal is a clear shortlist, not the biggest catalogue.

How does NoevaPet make money?

NoevaPet may receive a commission on tracked retailer purchases. This is explained openly.

Can I remove pets?

Yes. You can add and remove pet profiles at any time.

Can I upload a pet photo?

Yes. Each pet profile can have its own photo.

NoevaPet helps you find products that fit your pet, understand the differences and move to the retailer with one clear shopping plan.

NoevaPet may earn a commission on referred purchases. It does not change your price or the recommendation order. About transparency
